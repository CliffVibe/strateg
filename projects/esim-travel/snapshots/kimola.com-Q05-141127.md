Summary

Customer feedback reveals a consistent ease of eSIM installation and multi-country usability, contributing positively to user experience and travel convenience. However, significant variability exists in network reliability, speed, and coverage, often dependent on geographic location. Technical support and problem resolution are frequently inadequate, with many users encountering unresponsive or automated assistance, leading to unresolved activation and refund issues. App functionality suffers from stability and usability problems, including crashes and misleading information, further complicating user interactions. Pricing perceptions are divided, with some valuing convenience and others criticizing cost relative to local alternatives. While many users endorse the product for its straightforward setup and reliable connectivity in select regions, widespread challenges in activation, technical support, and network performance highlight critical areas requiring improvement to enhance overall satisfaction and trust.

This variability suggests that network reliability is highly dependent on geographic location and local infrastructure compatibility.
